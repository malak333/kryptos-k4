## Reviewed Facts

- The CIA artifact page identifies Kryptos as a courtyard artwork between the
  Original Headquarters Building and the New Headquarters Building.
- The page describes a copperplate screen with letters cut into it as the
  centerpiece of the artwork.
- The page attributes the work to James Sanborn and states that the artwork
  contains an enciphered message.
- The page describes multiple sculpture components beyond the copper screen,
  including granite, water, and petrified wood elements.
- The page states that three coded-message sections have been cracked while the
  fourth remains unsolved.
